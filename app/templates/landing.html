The Pipeline.

Three frictionless steps from delivery to final selection.

Phase 01

Studio Authentication

The photographer mounts their Google Drive directory via the dashboard. The system generates a unique, secure access ID for the client.

Phase 02

Client Curation

The client logs in and explores a cinematic, edge-rendered grid. They select their favorite moments across multiple events with zero lag.

Phase 03

Automated Extraction

Upon submission, FastFrame's backend triggers a batch process, silently dropping the selected high-resolution files into a finalized folder in the studio's Drive.
