            Setup Guide
            
Google Cloud Integration

        


        
        

            

                
                
                

                    
1. Enable Drive API

                    
Log in to the Google Cloud Console. Create a new Project. Use the top search bar to find "Google Drive API" and click the blue Enable button.

                


                
                

                    
2. Create Service Account

                    
Navigate to IAM & Admin > Service Accounts. Click "Create Service Account" at the top. Name it FastFrame Drive Sync and click Done. This acts as a robotic assistant that manages your files.

                


                
                

                    
3. Generate JSON & Share

                    
Click the three dots next to your new account. Go to Manage Keys > Add Key > Create New Key > JSON. Upload that downloaded file to FastFrame. 

CRITICAL: You must share your Google Drive photo folders with the generated Service Account Email as an "Editor".
